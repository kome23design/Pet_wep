Veterinary Assurance

10-Year Health Warranty & Clinical Policy

Prestige Paws NYC provides an unprecedented 10-Year Genetic Health Warranty backed by multi-panel DNA parentage screenings and certified USDA pediatric veterinary examinations.

Our Constitutional Guarantee

Unconditional Genetic Accountability

While commercial breeders often restrict health guarantees to a standard 14 or 30 days, Prestige Paws NYC extends protection across your dog's prime developmental years. Because our partner nurseries screen parentage through multi-generational DNA panels, we stand behind the constitutional genetic health of our companion animals for ten full years.

Covered Genetic & Congenital Conditions

10-Year Protection

If at any point during the ten (10) year period following placement your dog is diagnosed by a board-certified veterinary specialist with a severe hereditary or congenital condition that compromises life expectancy or quality of life, Prestige Paws NYC will provide financial remedies under our covenant. Covered conditions include:

Congenital Cardiac Defects

Subaortic stenosis, patent ductus arteriosus (PDA), severe mitral valve dysplasia, and congenital cardiomyopathy diagnosed via echocardiogram.

Hereditary Neurological Disorders

Degenerative myelopathy (DM), congenital epilepsy, hydrocephalus, and hereditary cerebellar ataxia confirmed by MRI/neurologist.

Hereditary Ocular Diseases

Progressive Retinal Atrophy (PRA-prcd) resulting in irreversible juvenile or mid-life blindness certified by a veterinary ophthalmologist.

Congenital Organ Failure

Congenital liver shunts (portosystemic shunts), hereditary renal dysplasia, or juvenile polycystic kidney disease verified by blood panels and ultrasound.

Mandatory Included Medical Documentation

Bound official records presented in your arrival folder:

Official USDA-APHIS Form 7001 Health Certificate: Completed within 5 days of transit by our licensed Doctor of Veterinary Medicine certifying freedom from infectious disease and travel fitness.
Vaccination & Preventative Health Passport: Exact dates, vaccine lot numbers, and dosages for DHPP, Bordetella, and broad-spectrum pediatric deworming protocols.
Universal ISO Microchip Identification & Transfer Form: Pre-registered with the 24/7 Universal Pet Recovery Database, enabling immediate international scanning and owner verification.
Pedigree & Multi-Generation Lineage Summary: Documented ancestry confirming purebred Maltese heritage or certified first-generation/second-generation Maltipoo crossbreed lineage.

5-Day Initial Adopter Veterinary Exam Requirement

To activate your 10-Year Genetic Health Warranty and establish an independent clinical baseline, the adopter must have the companion examined by a licensed Doctor of Veterinary Medicine within five (5) business days of arrival.

This initial exam provides total peace of mind for your family. If during this 5-day window your veterinarian discovers an undisclosed, pre-existing health issue, you have the right to reimbursement of reasonable clinical fees or placement adjustments in accordance with New York animal protection statutes.

Common Exclusions & Owner Responsibilities

The 10-Year Warranty specifically protects against inherited constitutional and genetic defects. It naturally excludes conditions resulting from environmental neglect or ordinary ownership risk:

  • Trauma & Accidents: Fractures, falls, ingestion of foreign objects, or car accidents.
  • Dietary Mismanagement: Hypoglycemia caused by skipped meals or obesity from excessive feeding.
  • Parasites & Common Environmental Exposure: Fleas, ticks, mites, or seasonal kennel cough contracted post-arrival in community dog parks.
  • Neglected Dental Care: Dental tartar or periodontal disease arising from failure to brush teeth.

Filing a Health Warranty Inquiry

In the rare event of a suspected genetic condition, your licensed veterinarian submits their clinical report, diagnostic imaging (radiographs, ultrasound, or blood panels), and statement directly to our Chief Veterinary Officer at veterinary@prestigepaws.nyc. We process all inquiries within 72 hours with compassion, professionalism, and fairness.
