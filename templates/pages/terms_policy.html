Legal Governance & Covenant

Placement Terms & Conditions

These terms establish the legal and ethical framework governing the reservation, veterinary clearance, and lifetime care of companion animals placed through Royal Maltipoos.

1. Legal Eligibility & Adopter Capacity

By initiating an adoption reservation or submitting payment, you represent and warrant that you are at least twenty-one (21) years of age, legally competent, and authorized by your building management, landlord, or condominium bylaws to maintain a canine companion at the delivery destination. You further represent that no member of your household has ever been convicted of animal cruelty, neglect, or welfare violations under federal, state, or municipal law.

2. Unique Living Animal Reservation & Anti-Speculation

Every puppy presented in our catalog is an individual living animal. Inventory reservation locks the pet to your order exclusively via transactional database locking. In accordance with ethical standards, Royal Maltipoos strictly prohibits bulk purchases, commercial resale, speculative purchasing, or adoption by unauthorized third-party brokers.

3. Pre-Travel Clinical Inspection & USDA Clearance

All placements are strictly contingent upon our examining veterinarian issuing a clean bill of health and an official USDA-APHIS Form 7001 certificate. In the event our clinical team detects any health anomaly before departure, travel will be postponed or refunded in full at the adopter's election.

4. Doorstep Handover & Transfer of Legal Custody

Legal title, custody, and risk of loss transfer to the adopter upon physical in-person handoff and written signature receipt verified by our licensed chaperone. Adopters must be present at the agreed delivery window and provide government-issued photo identification matching the order records.

5. Companion Pet Non-Breeding Covenant (Spay / Neuter)

All puppies placed by Royal Maltipoos are adopted strictly as companion family pets. Breeding rights are not conveyed unless explicitly granted under a separate written agreement. Adopters agree to have their puppy spayed or neutered by a licensed veterinarian between six (6) and twelve (12) months of age.

6. Lifetime Return & Zero-Shelter Covenant

Adopters covenant that under no circumstances will the animal ever be abandoned, surrendered to a municipal pound, sold at public auction, or given to an animal shelter. If you are ever unable to continue ownership, you must contact Royal Maltipoos immediately to facilitate safe return or private re-homing.

7. Governing Law & Jurisdiction

These terms shall be governed by, construed, and enforced in accordance with the substantive laws of the State of New York, without regard to conflicts of law principles. Any dispute arising out of this agreement shall be submitted exclusively to the state or federal courts located within the County of New York, State of New York.
