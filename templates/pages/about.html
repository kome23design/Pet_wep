Heritage & Compassionate Stewardship

The Royal Maltipoos Atelier

Founded in Manhattan, New York, Royal Maltipoos was conceived to transform the companion adoption experience into an atelier of uncompromising ethics, veterinary transparency, and dedicated family care.

Our Founding Ethos

A Reimagined Standard for Toy Companion Placement

For decades, the commercial pet industry in the United States has suffered from a painful dichotomy: either opaque online brokers hiding substandard mass-breeding operations, or informal local classifieds lacking basic genetic screening and veterinary oversight.

Royal Maltipoos was founded by canine geneticists and passionate animal welfare advocates to create a sanctuary of trust. We established a flagship Manhattan atelier at 450 Lexington Avenue where families can engage with fully vetted, ethically hand-reared companion puppies in a calm, hypoallergenic salon environment.

Every puppy in our program originates from an exclusive network of vetted, licensed partner nurseries that exceed all state and federal humane standards. We know every mother dog by name, enforce mandatory maternal rest periods, and raise our litters with the scientifically validated Puppy Culture Early Neurological Stimulation (ENS) protocol.

Guiding Principles

The 4 Pillars of Our Atelier

Principles that guide every placement decision we make.

Zero-Mill Covenant

We strictly refuse high-volume commercial mills. Partner nurseries must pass unannounced bi-annual inspections, provide indoor climate-controlled spaces, and provide outdoor enrichment runs.

Genetic Integrity

Sire and dam genetics are screened via multi-panel DNA testing (Paw Print Genetics, Embark) and Orthopedic Foundation for Animals (OFA) clearances before any breeding pairing is approved.

Veterinary Oversight

Pediatric clinical exams, complete core DHPP vaccination series, universal 15-digit ISO microchip registration, and official USDA-APHIS 7001 interstate certificates accompany every companion.

Lifetime Rehoming

If unforeseen life changes prevent an adopter from continuing care, Royal Maltipoos takes the companion back into our private network unconditionally. No pet of ours ever enters a municipal shelter.
