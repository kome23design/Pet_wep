Our Sacred Covenant

Animal Welfare Standards & Manifesto

At Prestige Paws NYC, the emotional, genetic, and physical well-being of our companion animals is our paramount covenant. Every decision—from partner nursery selection to doorstep handover—is dictated strictly by animal welfare.

Our Uncompromising Covenant

The 7 Pillars of Prestige Paws Stewardship

Commercial mass-breeding has no place in a compassionate society. We adhere to seven non-negotiable standards that guarantee every puppy is raised with profound respect, human love, and medical rigor.

1
Maternal Dignity

Zero-Tolerance Mass Breeding & Maternal Rest

We categorically oppose puppy mills, cage-bound breeding facilities, and brokers who commodify animals. All partner nurseries in our exclusive network are small-scale family ateliers subject to quarterly unannounced inspections by our veterinary compliance team.

• Mandatory Breeding Limits: Dams are limited to a maximum of four litters throughout their lifetime and must observe mandatory natural resting cycles between pregnancies.

• Living Environment: No cages or wire flooring. Dogs reside in climate-controlled indoor nurseries with immediate access to grassed outdoor socialization yards.

• Lifetime Retirement: When retired, parent dogs remain beloved permanent family companions in their nursery homes or are placed in pre-screened retirement families.

2
Neuro-Development

Early Neurological Stimulation (ENS) & Puppy Culture

From days 3 through 16 of life, our caretakers perform the scientifically validated Bio Sensor ENS protocol developed by military canine behaviorists. This five-step gentle tactile stimulation produces puppies with measurably stronger cardiovascular performance, elevated disease resistance, and greater tolerance to environmental stress.

Tactile Stimulation: Gentle tickling between toes with sterile swabs.
Thermal Stimulation: Brief exposure to cool, clean surface cloths.
Positional Balance: Supine head-erect and head-down gentle holding.
Domestic Sound Desensitization: Vacuum, doorbell, and city ambient soundtrack habituation.
3
Genetic Screening

Multi-Panel DNA & Orthopedic Clearances

True animal welfare begins with genetic health before conception occurs. All breeding sires and dams undergo complete genetic disease sequencing (through Paw Print Genetics or Embark) and Orthopedic Foundation for Animals (OFA) evaluations.

  • Patellar Luxation Clearance: Orthopedic evaluation of stifles to eliminate genetic slipping kneecaps common in toy breeds.
  • Degenerative Myelopathy (DM): Testing to prevent inherited spinal cord deterioration.
  • Progressive Retinal Atrophy (PRA-prcd): Complete eye clearances to prevent hereditary blindness.
  • Von Willebrand's Disease (vWD): DNA panel ensuring healthy blood clotting factors.
4
Holistic Pediatric Care

Organic Nutrition & Natural Weaning

We never artificially separate puppies from their mothers prematurely. Puppies remain with their dam until naturally weaned between 7 and 8 weeks, ensuring crucial maternal antibodies and critical bite inhibition lessons taught by littermates.

Transition nutrition consists of certified human-grade, holistic puppy formulas enriched with DHA for brain development, prebiotics for digestive flora, and balanced omega fatty acids.

5
Veterinary Certification

Clinical Pre-Departure Examination (USDA-APHIS)

Within five days prior to placement, every companion undergoes a rigorous physical exam by a licensed Doctor of Veterinary Medicine (DVM). The clinical audit encompasses:

Cardiac Auscultation
Pulmonary Screening
Ophthalmic Audit
Otic Canal & Tympanum
Patellar Alignment
15-Digit Microchip Scan
6
Chaperoned Travel

Zero-Cargo Hand-Delivery Protocol

Commercial airline cargo holds subject animals to terrifying decibels, toxic ramp exhaust, and temperature extremes. We refuse cargo shipping under any circumstance.

Our certified flight nannies cradle the puppy in-cabin directly under passenger seating. For ground travel, our custom Mercedes-Benz Sprinters maintain hospital-grade HEPA air filtration and continuous 70°F climate regulation, with mandatory 3-hour hydration and stretch breaks.

7
Lifelong Protection

Lifetime Return & Zero-Shelter Covenant

Our commitment to each animal does not terminate upon delivery. If an adopter's health, housing, or personal circumstances ever prevent continued care at any point in the dog's life, Prestige Paws NYC takes the dog back unconditionally.

We immediately take custody, conduct a full veterinary screening at our expense, and arrange private rehoming through our vetted adopter registry. No animal placed by Prestige Paws NYC will ever enter a municipal shelter or rescue facility.
