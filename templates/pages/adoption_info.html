Pre-Arrival Preparation

The Essential New Puppy Shopping List

Procure before arrival day

Sanctuary & Sleep

  • 24″ Wire Crate: With divider panel so the space grows with your toy puppy.
  • Playpen (X-Pen): 30″ height for safe daytime containment in your living room.
  • Washable Donut Bed: Orthopedic plush bolsters for comforting tactile security.
  • Snuggle Puppy with Heartbeat: Reassuring device mimicking maternal heartbeat.

Dining & Health

  • Shallow Stainless Steel Bowls: Heavy-bottomed, non-skid to prevent tip-overs.
  • Transition Kibble: Starter holistic blend provided in your Prestige welcome pack.
  • Nutri-Cal Gel: High-calorie nutritional supplement preventing toy hypoglycemia.
  • Natural Probiotic Chews: Supporting delicate post-travel gastrointestinal balance.

Coat & Hygiene

  • Slicker Brush & Greyhound Comb: Essential daily tools preventing coat matting.
  • Tear-Stain Cleaning Pads: Alcohol-free cucumber/chamomile wipes for Maltese eyes.
  • Enzymatic Puppy Cleaner: Nature's Miracle or Rocco & Roxie for accidental messes.
  • Grass Potty Patch or Pads: Designated indoor housebreaking area.
Safety First

Room-by-Room Puppy-Proofing Blueprint

Curious toy puppies explore the world entirely with their mouths.

Living Room & Common Areas

  • Electrical & Phone Cords: Thread charging cords through plastic cord protectors or elevate off the floor.
  • Houseplants: Remove toxic flora such as lilies, philodendrons, pothos, sago palms, and aloe vera.
  • Small Objects: Inspect rugs daily for rubber bands, coins, hair pins, sewing needles, and dropped medication.
  • Furniture Gaps: Block narrow crawlspaces under sofas where a 2-pound puppy could become wedged.

Kitchen & Dining Areas

  • Trash Cans: Install step-cans with locking lids to prevent access to bones or toxic food scraps.
  • Toxic Human Foods: Zero access to grapes, raisins, onions, garlic, chocolate, macadamia nuts, and xylitol/birch sugar.
  • Cabinet Latches: Secure lower cabinets holding dish detergents, sponges, and floor cleaners.
  • Spills: Wipe cooking grease or onion bits off floor tiles immediately before puppy licking occurs.

Bathrooms & Laundry

  • Toilet Lids Down: Keep toilet lids closed at all times to prevent drowning hazard for toy puppies.
  • Medication Bottles: Store all prescriptions and vitamin gummies inside closed upper medicine cabinets.
  • Toilet Paper & Floss: Dental floss is a catastrophic linear foreign body hazard; keep wastebaskets covered.

Balconies, Terraces & Stairs

  • Balcony Railings: Standard balcony spindles have 4-inch gaps. Install transparent plexiglass or mesh guard rails.
  • Stairway Gates: Use freestanding pressure gates to prevent tumbling down steep steps during early weeks.
  • Elevator Doors: Always hold toy puppies in your arms when entering or exiting Manhattan elevator cabs.
Nursery Transition Protocol

The Crucial First 72 Hours at Home

Your puppy is experiencing an entirely new sensory world—new voices, new scents, and the separation from their litter. Follow this calm schedule for optimal bonding:

Day 1: Arrival & Decompression

Upon arrival and delivery, take the puppy directly to your indoor potty grass patch before touring the home. Offer room-temperature water and a small meal. Keep the environment quiet with soothing classical music. Avoid inviting extended guests today.

Day 2: Routine Establishment

Introduce predictable 4-hour eating and elimination cycles. Practice gentle 5-minute handling sessions: touching paws, inspecting ears, and stroking the coat with your fingers. Introduce the designated exercise pen for safe independent rest.

Day 3: Confidence & Bonding

Begin gentle name recognition training using tiny bites of boiled chicken or puppy training treats. Familiarize the puppy with their soft sleep crate by scattering treats inside with the door propped wide open.

Clinical Nutrition

Pediatric Feeding Guidelines & Hypoglycemia Prevention

Toy Maltipoo and Maltese puppies possess tiny digestive systems with minimal glycogen storage in their livers. To prevent juvenile hypoglycemia (low blood sugar), adhere strictly to this regimen:

Meal Frequency 3 to 4 Meals Daily

Divide daily caloric ration into 8:00 AM, 12:00 PM, 4:00 PM, and 8:00 PM feedings.

Hydration Filtered Fresh Water

Change water bowl twice daily. In hard water areas, use filtered or spring water to minimize tear staining.

Emergency Sugar Gel Nutri-Cal or Pure Honey

If the puppy appears lethargic, rub a pea-sized dab of Nutri-Cal or pure maple syrup on their gums immediately.
