1. Adoption & Reservation Process

Atelier Protocol

How does the reservation process work?

When you select an available companion online, our database-level transaction lock immediately secures the puppy, transitioning their status from Available to Reserved. You can place your deposit or full payment securely through our Stripe-encrypted checkout. Following confirmation, our concierge desk contacts you within 24 hours to coordinate pre-travel veterinary inspection and schedule your delivery window.

Can multiple people accidentally purchase the same puppy?

No. Living companions are singular, unique beings. Our backend database enforces atomic row locking (select_for_update) during checkout. Once a customer enters the payment confirmation phase, no other user can add that specific animal to their cart or finalize a purchase.

Can I meet the puppy in person before finalizing adoption?

Yes. Prospective adopters in the New York metropolitan area may book a private viewing consultation at our Manhattan atelier located at 450 Lexington Avenue. For clients residing out of state, our concierge provides private one-on-one live video appointments where you can watch the puppy interact with toys, walk, and engage with caretakers in real time.

What is the minimum age at which a puppy can go home?

In strict compliance with veterinary guidelines and Federal animal welfare statutes, no puppy is placed prior to completing eight (8) full weeks of age. Many of our toy companions remain in nursery care through 10 to 12 weeks to ensure optimal pediatric development, maternal weaning, and early house manners.

2. Breeds, Temperament & Hypoallergenic Care

Maltipoo & Maltese

Are Maltipoos and Maltese truly hypoallergenic?

Both breeds possess hair rather than fur, meaning they lack the dense shedding undercoat that disperses dry dander into indoor air. While no dog is 100% allergenic-free (since saliva and skin cells carry small amounts of Can f 1 protein), Maltipoos and Maltese are widely regarded by canine immunologists as the most allergy-friendly companions available. Families with mild-to-moderate dog allergies routinely experience zero symptoms.

What is the difference between a Maltipoo and a Maltese?

The Maltese is a purebred ancient toy companion with a silky snow-white coat, gentle regal demeanor, and an adult weight typically between 4 and 7 lbs. The Maltipoo is a purposeful designer hybrid of a Maltese and a Toy Poodle, known for wavy or curly teddy-bear fleece coats in apricot, champagne, or cream, acute intelligence, and slightly higher athletic energy.

How often do these breeds require grooming?

Because their hair continuously grows like human hair, daily 3-to-5 minute brushing with a slicker brush and metal greyhound comb is recommended to prevent tangles. Professional grooming (a bath, sanitary trim, and puppy cut) is typically scheduled every 6 to 8 weeks.

3. Health Standards & 10-Year Warranty

Veterinary Guarantee

What does the 10-Year Genetic Warranty cover?

Our warranty covers all life-threatening hereditary and congenital defects, including congenital heart disease (subaortic stenosis, PDA), severe hip/elbow dysplasia, degenerative myelopathy (DM), hereditary blindness (PRA), and congenital organ failure (such as portosystemic liver shunts). If confirmed by a veterinary specialist, we provide financial reimbursement or medical allowances.

What vaccines will my puppy have received upon arrival?

Your puppy will arrive with age-appropriate core vaccinations (DHPP: Canine Distemper, Infectious Hepatitis, Parvovirus, Parainfluenza), intranasal Bordetella, and a complete pediatric deworming schedule. All dates and manufacturer lot numbers are recorded in your included official health passport.

Is the microchip pre-registered?

Yes. Every puppy is implanted with an ISO standard 15-digit microchip readable by all international veterinary scanners. The registration in the universal 24/7 pet recovery database is pre-paid for lifetime protection with zero recurring annual fees.

4. Chaperoned Hand-Delivery Logistics

Nationwide Chaperoned

How does chaperoned hand-delivery work?

We categorically never ship animals in airline cargo. For long-distance placements, a certified flight nanny travels with your puppy in-cabin under passenger seating, ensuring continuous comfort and hydration. For regional placements, our dedicated Mercedes-Benz Sprinter vans provide climate-controlled 70°F travel with mandatory 3-hour comfort stops. The chaperone personally hands your puppy directly to you at your residence.

How much does delivery cost?

We maintain a transparent flat fee of $250.00 USD for chaperoned companion delivery across all contiguous United States. This flat fee covers the chaperone, airline pet in-cabin ticketing or Mercedes Sprinter transit, sanitized travel crate, and nursery welcome kit.

What happens if severe weather occurs on delivery day?

Animal welfare is our paramount covenant. If extreme blizzards, ground frost advisories, or high temperatures exceeding 85°F develop along the transit corridor, travel is rescheduled automatically to ensure zero risk to puppy health. Our concierge coordinates alternative dates immediately.

5. Lifelong Support & Rehoming Covenant

Lifetime Care

What support do you provide after my puppy arrives?

Our placement relationship is lifelong. Adopters receive direct access to our Manhattan concierge desk for ongoing guidance regarding pediatric nutrition, teething, crate training, grooming recommendations, and socialization advice.

What is your lifetime rehoming policy?

If unforeseen life changes (such as illness, divorce, or relocation) prevent an adopter from continuing care at any point in the dog's life, Royal Maltipoos guarantees to take the dog back into our private network unconditionally. No Royal Maltipoos dog ever enters a shelter.
