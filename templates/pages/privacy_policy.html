Privacy & Data Security Policy

Royal Maltipoos is committed to safeguarding the privacy, confidentiality, and data sovereignty of our private clients, adopters, and nursery partners.

1. Scope and Controller Information

This Privacy Policy governs the collection, storage, and utilization of personal data by Royal Maltipoos (“Royal Maltipoos”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) through our website, concierge desks, and Manhattan atelier located at 450 Lexington Avenue, Suite 1800, New York, NY 10017.

2. Categories of Information Collected

In order to coordinate legitimate companion pet placement, official USDA health documentation, and chaperoned hand-delivery, we collect:

  • Client Identification: Legal full name, email address, direct telephone number, and government-verified contact details.
  • Residence & Destination Records: Physical street address, apartment/suite number, doorman/gatehouse access instructions, and preferred delivery windows.
  • Pet Lineage & Microchip Registration Data: Registered owner details transmitted to the 24/7 Universal Pet Recovery Database upon placement.
  • Technical & Session Logs: IP address, browser type, device identifiers, and session tokens utilized solely for CSRF protection and shopping cart persistence.

3. Payment & Financial Security (PCI-DSS Level 1)

We categorically never store, process, or retain raw credit card numbers, CVV codes, or full banking credentials on our local servers. All electronic payments are processed through Stripe's certified PCI-DSS Level 1 encrypted payment architecture. Data transmissions are encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3) with 256-bit cryptographic keys.

4. Third-Party Disclosures & Strict Confidentiality

Royal Maltipoos will never sell, lease, or monetize client personal data to marketing brokers, data aggregators, or unauthorized commercial third parties. Information is disclosed exclusively to:

  • Licensed Examining Veterinarians: To generate legally mandated USDA-APHIS interstate travel health certificates.
  • Assigned Chaperones & Flight Nannies: Solely for the operational coordination of door-to-door hand delivery.
  • Microchip Registration Authorities: To link the 15-digit ISO microchip to your name in universal lost-pet recovery networks.

5. Adopter Rights & Data Subject Access (SHIELD & CCPA)

In compliance with the New York Stop Hacks and Improve Electronic Data Security (SHIELD) Act and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), you retain the right to request access to the personal records held in your client dossier, request correction of inaccurate addresses, or request account deletion, subject only to mandatory veterinary record retention requirements mandated by Federal animal welfare laws.

6. Privacy Inquiries & Data Protection Officer

Inquiries regarding data confidentiality or account management should be addressed to our Privacy Compliance Directorate via email at support@royalmaltipoos.shop or via written correspondence to Royal Maltipoos, 450 Lexington Avenue, Suite 1800, New York, NY 10017.
