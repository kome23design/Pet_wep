Consumer Safeguards & Fairness

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Our financial policies are designed with supreme fairness to adopters and unwavering dedication to the health and safety of our living companion animals.

1. Pre-Travel Health Clearance 100% Full Refund Guarantee

If at any point during the pre-departure clinical examination our licensed veterinarian determines that a puppy is unfit for travel or displays any pre-existing health anomaly, the adopter is immediately notified. You retain the absolute right to:

  • An immediate, unconditional 100% full refund of all placement fees and delivery charges.
  • Or, a priority transfer of your full payment to an upcoming nursery litter of your choice.

2. Adopter-Initiated Cancellation Prior to Dispatch

We understand that unforeseen family emergencies can arise. Adopters may cancel their reservation up to 48 hours prior to scheduled courier or flight nanny departure.

Upon receiving written cancellation notice, the puppy's status is promptly restored to Available in our catalog, and your payment is refunded via the original payment method, less an administrative and veterinary holding fee of $150 to cover nursery preparation and initial pediatric exam costs.

3. Severe Weather Rescheduling Protocol

If extreme weather, blizzards, sub-zero ground freezes, or airline heat embargos require travel postponement, dispatches are rescheduled to the earliest safe window. Because postponement protects the animal's life, weather delays do not constitute breach of contract or entitle a cancellation penalty.

4. Initial 5-Day Adopter Veterinary Exam Recourse

If your licensed veterinarian examines the puppy within five (5) business days of handover and certifies in writing that the animal possesses a serious pre-existing health condition undisclosed prior to departure, Prestige Paws NYC will either cover reasonable veterinary medical treatment or provide a full replacement companion under New York General Business Law Article 35-D (“Pet Purchaser Protection Act”).

5. Refund Processing Timelines

All approved refunds are submitted through Stripe within two (2) business days of authorization. Depending on your financial institution or credit card issuer, settled funds typically appear on your billing statement within 3 to 7 business days.
