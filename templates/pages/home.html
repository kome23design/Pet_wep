The Prestige Covenant

Why Discerning Adopters Trust Prestige Paws

We adhere to the most rigorous ethical breeding, veterinary inspection, and placement protocols in the United States.

USDA Clinical Exam

Every puppy completes a comprehensive nose-to-tail clinical inspection by licensed veterinarians, verified with official USDA-APHIS health certification within 5 days of placement.

10-Year Genetic Warranty

Our industry-leading warranty protects against hereditary and congenital conditions, supported by multi-panel DNA parentage screenings and OFA health clearances.

Chaperoned Hand Delivery

We never place animals in cargo. All travel is personally chaperoned in-cabin by certified flight nannies or in dedicated climate-controlled Mercedes-Benz Sprinters.

Lifetime Support Covenant

Complimentary direct access to our Manhattan canine nutritionists, behaviorists, 24/7 universal microchip recovery network, and lifetime rehoming guarantee.

First-Class Pet Transport

Chaperoned Hand-Delivery to Your Door

Commercial cargo shipping is stressful and dangerous for young toy puppies. That is why Prestige Paws NYC maintains an uncompromising covenant: every single puppy travels strictly in the direct personal care of a certified chaperone.

In-Cabin Certified Flight Nannies

For destinations requiring air travel, our dedicated flight nanny cradles your puppy in an FAA-approved soft carrier in the aircraft passenger cabin, monitoring hydration and comfort throughout flight.

Dedicated Climate-Controlled Sprinters

For regional and East Coast placements, our custom Mercedes-Benz Sprinters maintain a steady 70°F environment with medical-grade air filtration and scheduled 3-hour comfort stops.

Complete Nursery Transition Package Included

Your puppy arrives with an organic puppy nutrition supply, mother's scent blanket, microchip transfer certificate, vaccination passport, and official health dossier.
